WI Effigy Mound Data Digitization

This interactive web map facilitates the exploration of Late Woodland Effigy Mound distributions across Wisconsin. Developed in coordination with the Office of the State Archaeologist, this tool serves as a resource for spatial notation and landscape analysis.

Please note: To ensure the protection and preservation of these cultural resources, all site locations have been generalized for visualization.
